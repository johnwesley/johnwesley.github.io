At Trumid, a corporate bond trading venue, I lead identity and access security across AWS, Azure/Entra ID, GCP, GitHub and Kubernetes: replacing static credentials with short-lived workload identity, moving every access grant into reviewed infrastructure-as-code, and governing AI agents as identities with their own least-privilege boundaries.

I ship that work with agentic tooling behind human review gates, and I built the guardrails that make that safe.
